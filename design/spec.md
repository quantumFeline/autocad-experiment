# Design

This is the client requirements for the **realistic, custom-designed 3d-printed thumb prosthesis**.

## Overview

The overall design should be functional and pleasing for the view, having realistic proportions and clean curves. At the same time, it should *not* aim for being life-like - rather it should make use of unnatural colours and visible hinges, giving the prosthesis a cybernetic, futurustic look.

As the length of the stump is not enough to ensure firm positioning, the prosthesis is held in place via a simple clothing wrap around the hand. The wrap is attached to the prosthesis via two *flexible* plates at the inner and outer palm sides of the prosthesis, with the slits that allow for the fabric to be slid through them.

The insides not in direct contact with the base, hinge, or outer side should be hollow to ensure the prothesis is light.

The colour scheme is, tentatively, white with black details (black decorative nail, black edges of the hinge disks).

## Base

The base is triangular-ish, with soft rounding, around 15 mm at its highest point, and is asymmetrical; the base is roughly 30 mm wide, with the highest point roughly one third (i.e. 10mm, measuring at the base) away from the size that approches the other fingers. In practice, the print should leave 1-2mm of space as the insides will be lined with soft foam for the user's comfort.

From the side view or top view, the base should be slightly wider at the outer (wrist) side - ~25 mm with the inner (fingers) side ~15mm.

## Proximal phalange

The proximal phalange should be longer at the outer side in the front view and the outer palm side in the side view, allowing some space for bend, i.e. flexing the stump without plastic cutting into the palm.

## Joint hinge

The design has a single hinge that imitates the interphalangeal joint. The movement of the metacarpal phalangeal joint should, via combination of pressure and thin wires, translate to the interphalangeal joint at roughly the same angle without requiring too much extra force from the user.

The hinge is clearly visible, having two round, bicolour disks at the side views of the prosthesis. They are roughly 5mm in diameter.

## Distal phalange

The distal phalange should be about 25mm long and feature a decorative nail (with its outline carved) around 8mm long.In should have a soft curve resembling one of a real thumb.
